AudienceKit, and every app built on it, including **Hot Mess** and **Hack Different**, is operated by Love Wins Media, Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy applies to all of them, on every platform.

## Zero tolerance

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for the sexual exploitation of children in any form. That includes grooming, sextortion, trafficking, contacting a minor for any sexual purpose, and sexualized content involving minors, whether real, drawn or generated. It applies to photos, chat messages, profiles, posts and any other content.

## Adults only

All of our services are for adults 18 and older, and some venues are 21 and older. We remove any account we learn belongs to someone under 18.

## Where images come from

AudienceKit hosts event photos, talent and profile photos, and posters.

- **Members** can add event photos only with the in-app camera, and only while they are at the venue. They can't upload from their photo library.
- **Professional event photos** come from photographers working with the venue or community.
- **Talent and venue photos and posters** are added by the venues, organizers and talent who run those listings.
- **Chat** rooms are moderated. Moderators and our staff can remove messages and ban accounts, and automated checks can flag messages for review.

## Hash-matching of uploaded images

We are integrating hash-matching technology, such as Microsoft PhotoDNA, into AudienceKit. Once it is live, every image uploaded to AudienceKit or Hot Mess, including event photos, talent and profile photos, posters and images shared in chat, is checked against lists of hashes of known CSAM as soon as it is uploaded. A match is hidden from everyone at once and goes to our child safety reviewers. We don't use any system that guesses what a picture shows; matching uses only lists of known material.

## What we do when we find CSAM

- We remove the content and hide it from everyone at once.
- We permanently ban the accounts involved.
- A trained reviewer looks at every case, and we report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, as US law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).
- We preserve the material and the related account information in restricted storage for as long as the law requires, and longer when law enforcement asks us to. Only our designated child safety reviewers can open it.

## Report a concern

- **In the app:** use **Report** on the message, photo or person.
- **By email:** write to [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Child%20safety%20report) with the subject "Child safety report". This mailbox is monitored.

We treat child safety reports as our top priority and review every report within 24 hours. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the US you can also report directly to the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/).

## Law enforcement

We cooperate with law enforcement investigating child sexual exploitation, and respond to valid legal process and preservation requests. Law enforcement can reach us at [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Law%20enforcement%20request).

## Appeals

If you think your content or account was removed by mistake, email [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Appeal) with the subject "Appeal". A person will review it. We can't restore content we have confirmed as CSAM.
