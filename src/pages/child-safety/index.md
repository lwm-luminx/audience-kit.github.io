Love Wins Media, Inc. ("we", "us") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards apply to AudienceKit and every app built on it, including **Hot Mess** and **Hack Different**, on every platform.

## Who can use the apps

The apps are for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We remove accounts we learn belong to anyone under 18.

## What's prohibited

You may not use the apps to create, upload, share, request or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or to groom, sexualise, sextort or traffic a child, or to contact a minor for any sexual purpose. This covers chat messages, photos, profiles, posts and any other content.

## How we prevent and act on it

- **In-app reporting:** every chat message has **Report** and **Block**. Reports go to the community's moderators and to us.
- **Moderation:** chat rooms are moderated. Moderators and our staff can remove messages and ban accounts. Automated checks can flag messages for review.
- **Action:** when we find or are told about CSAE, we remove the content, permanently ban the accounts involved, and keep the evidence the law requires.
- **Reporting to authorities:** we report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, and cooperate with law enforcement, as required by law.

## Report a concern

In the app, use **Report** on the message or person. You can also email **[hello@audiencekit.com](mailto:hello@audiencekit.com?subject=Child%20safety%20report)** with the subject "Child safety report". If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the US you can also report to the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/).

## Contact

Our child safety point of contact is reachable at [hello@audiencekit.com](mailto:hello@audiencekit.com). We review child safety reports as a priority.
