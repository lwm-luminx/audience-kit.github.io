You can ask us to delete your account and everything we hold about you at any time. This applies to AudienceKit and every app built on it, including **Hot Mess**.

## Delete your account and data

1. Email [hello@audiencekit.com](mailto:hello@audiencekit.com?subject=Delete%20my%20data) from the email address on your Facebook account, with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which app you use, for example Hot Mess.
2. We'll reply to confirm the request came from you. If we can't match your email, we'll ask for the name on your Facebook profile.
3. We delete your data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

## Remove the app from Facebook

You can also stop an app receiving any more information from Facebook:

1. In Facebook, open **Settings & privacy**, then **Settings**.
2. Choose **Apps and websites**.
3. Find the app, for example Hot Mess or AudienceKit, and choose **Remove**.
4. Facebook may offer to tell us to delete what we hold. If you tick that box, we'll delete it as described above.

## What gets deleted

- your profile: name, email address, profile picture and the other details Facebook shared;
- your Facebook sign-in tokens and any Page access you connected;
- your sign-in sessions and tokens, and the location history recorded with them;
- your likes, friend connections, RSVPs, check-ins, safety pings and messages to venues.

We may keep a record that we deleted your account, and anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as we must. Public listings for venues, events and people, which come from public sources and aren't about you as a user, aren't affected.

To have an event photo of you removed, email us a link to the photo.

Read our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for more about the information we collect.
