You can ask us to delete your account and everything we hold about you at any time. This applies to AudienceKit and every app built on it, including **Hot Mess** and **Hack Different**, both published by Love Wins Media, Inc.

## Delete your account in the app

In **Hot Mess** or **Hack Different**, open your profile and choose **Delete Account**, then confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away, and you're signed out on every device.

One AudienceKit account covers every app built on it. Deleting it from any app deletes it everywhere: your Hot Mess and Hack Different profiles, and any venue or admin roles you hold in the AudienceKit console.

## Delete your account and data by email

1. Email [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Delete%20my%20data) from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which app you use, for example Hot Mess or Hack Different.
2. We'll reply to confirm the request came from you. If we can't match your email, we'll ask for the name on your profile.
3. We delete your data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

## Remove the app from Facebook

You can also stop an app receiving any more information from Facebook:

1. In Facebook, open **Settings & privacy**, then **Settings**.
2. Choose **Apps and websites**.
3. Find the app, for example Hot Mess or AudienceKit, and choose **Remove**.
4. Facebook may offer to tell us to delete what we hold. If you tick that box, we'll delete it as described above.

## What gets deleted

- your profile: name, email address, profile picture and the other details your sign-in provider (Facebook, Apple, Google or Discord) shared;
- your Facebook sign-in tokens and any Page access you connected;
- your sign-in sessions and tokens, and the location history recorded with them;
- your likes, friend connections, RSVPs, check-ins, safety pings, chat messages and messages to venues.

We may keep a record that we deleted your account, and anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as we must. Public listings for venues, events and people, which come from public sources and aren't about you as a user, aren't affected.

To have an event photo of you removed, email us a link to the photo.

Read our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for more about the information we collect.
