We want AudienceKit and every app built on it, including **Hot Mess** and **Hack Different**, to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, larger text, voice control, a keyboard or a switch.

## What we aim for

Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA on our websites, and to follow Apple's and Google's accessibility guidance in our apps. That means text alternatives for images, readable contrast, support for larger text sizes, labels that VoiceOver and TalkBack can read, and controls that work without fine motor precision.

## Where we are

We haven't finished a full accessibility audit yet, so some parts of the apps and sites will fall short of that goal. We fix problems as we find them or hear about them. Some content comes from venues, organizers and other members, such as posters and photos, and may not have a text description.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something doesn't work for you, or you need information from one of our apps or sites in another format, email [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Accessibility) with the subject "Accessibility". Tell us which app or page, what you were trying to do, and the device and assistive technology you use if you're happy to share it. We reply to every message and will work with you on a fix or another way to get what you need.
