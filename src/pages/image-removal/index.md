If an intimate image or video of you was shared in AudienceKit or an app built on it, including **Hot Mess** and **Hack Different**, without your consent, tell us and we will remove it. This covers real images and ones that were digitally made or altered to look like you. You don't need an account to ask.

## How to ask

**In the app:** use **Report** on the photo, message or person and choose **Intimate image shared without consent**.

**By email:** write to [rick@lovewins.media](mailto:rick@lovewins.media?subject=Intimate%20image%20removal) with the subject "Intimate image removal". Someone acting for you, with your permission, can write instead. Include:

1. Where the image is, so we can find it: the app, the venue, event or chat room, who posted it and roughly when, or a link or screenshot. Please don't send us the image itself if you can describe where it is.
2. A short statement that the image shows you, and that you did not consent to it being shared.
3. How to reach you.
4. Your name typed at the end as your signature, or the name and signature of the person acting for you.

## What we do

- We remove an image named in a valid request as soon as we can, and no later than 48 hours after we receive the request.
- We make reasonable efforts to find and remove copies of the same image elsewhere in our apps.
- We act on the account that shared it under our [terms](/terms/), which can mean a permanent ban.
- We tell you when it's done, using the contact details you gave us.

We keep what you send us private and use it only to handle your request and to meet our legal duties.

## If the person in the image is under 18

That is child sexual abuse material. Report it to us the same way and we will treat it under our [Child Safety & CSAM Policy](/child-safety/), which includes a report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. NCMEC's [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) service can also help stop the image spreading on other platforms.

## Other help

- If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
- In the US, the Cyber Civil Rights Initiative runs a helpline for people whose intimate images were shared without consent, at [cybercivilrights.org](https://cybercivilrights.org/).
- For adults, [StopNCII.org](https://stopncii.org/) can help stop an image being shared on other platforms that take part.
